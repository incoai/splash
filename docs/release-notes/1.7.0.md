# Splash 1.7.0

Splash 1.7.0 verifies each lane's draft as a tree of up to 16 rows, with chains the prompt's own text proposes, in the decode forward the prompt runs; chooses each cycle's rows by the costs the start measures; keeps cached GDN states as int8 codes, a third of their size; and brings every change of 1.6.2, which was not released on its own (see [1.6.2](1.6.2.md)).

## Speed

Against 1.6.2, as speed (higher is faster), runs alternating 1.6.2, 1.7.0, 1.7.0, 1.6.2, each on a rested machine.

- **Served, 64 GB M5 Pro**, decode per token at one lane and four: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MLX 1.14× and 1.11×; Qwen3.8-27B GGUF 1.22× and about 1.15×; Ternary-Bonsai-2-27B 1.24× and about 1.33×; Flint 1.01× and 1.12×; Qwen3.8-Flash-Next with room for its experts 1.14× and 1.28× at 2K, 1.00× to 1.12× at 31K. At 2K the first token comes sooner in each of them, 1.01× to 1.47×.
- **Real agent sessions**, recorded pi and Codex sessions replayed on Qwen3.8-27B (62 requests, prompts of 2K to 43K tokens, mostly tool calls): each request 1.23× faster at the median, decode 1.46×; answers of 64 tokens or fewer 1.19×.
- **A conversation growing from 2K to 32K tokens**, decode per token at each turn: 1.09× to 1.19× on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B and 1.03× to 1.24× on Qwen3.8-27B at one lane; 1.07× to 1.17× at four lanes.
- **Other Macs**, decode per token at one to four lanes: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B UD-Q2_K_XL on a 24 GB M6 1.11× to 1.24×; Ternary-Bonsai-2-27B there 1.21× to 1.37×; Qwen3.6-35B-A3B UD-IQ1_M on a 16 GB M4 1.09× to 1.32×.
- **Experts streamed from the SSD**: Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on the M6 with a 6.6 GB store 1.01× to 1.07× at one lane, 1.08× at two and as fast as 1.6.2 at three and four; Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on the M4 with a 4.6 GB store as fast as 1.6.2 to 1.05× at one lane and 1.05× to 1.07× at two to four; Flash-Next on a 48 GB M5 Pro as fast as 1.6.2 to 1.16×.
- **Slower than 1.6.2 in three measured cells**, each where a cycle's step takes rows that do not pay: Qwen3.8-27B MLX on a 31K-token code prompt with a 20-token answer, one lane, 0.986× to 0.988× a token (the request 1.01×, its first token 1.09×), as its third cycle widens to 16 rows on an expected 9.5 tokens and keeps 7; Qwen3.6-35B-A3B GGUF on the same prompt's 383-token answer, one lane, 0.985×, on another answer than 1.6.2's (on a 71-token answer both write at two lanes, 1.29×); and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on a counting prompt at four lanes, 0.973×, verifying 8.02 rows a lane a cycle against 7.20 for the same 6.40 tokens. A step chosen from the last cycle's chances lets the draft run while the host builds the target's command; rows held to this cycle's estimates, as a streamed-expert target's are, come in a later release.

## Decode

- **Trees.** The draft grows each lane's rows into a tree over its candidates, best first by the chance the target accepts each row's path, and the target verifies every row along its own path, up to 16 rows a lane.
- **Prompt lookup.** The longest earlier occurrence of a request's latest tokens proposes up to 15 tokens that followed it, as a chain beside the draft's tree.
- **One decode forward.** A decode step runs through the prompt's layer loop as a chunk whose sequences are its lanes; a layer's attention takes 3 dispatches where 1.6.2 took 5.
- **Sampled lanes stay exact**: a sampled row's children are drawn without replacement and tested in draw order against the target's residual (recursive rejection sampling).
- **Each cycle's rows** follow the step costs the start measures for its model and Mac, with a cost a pass over each lane's history fitted while serving; a target whose experts stream takes its rows on the device by a floor on their estimates, from the cost line its cycles fit.
- **A cycle's draft runs while the host builds its target's command**: 0.3 to 0.5 ms less a cycle.
- **The draft's head** reads only the rows of the Qwen vocabulary's English, Chinese and chat tokens while a step's text stays in them: 1.2% to 1.7% less GPU time a step.

## Caches

- **Cached GDN states are int8 codes** in blocks of 32 with bf16 scales: a Qwen3.8-27B state takes 46.9 MiB where it took 152.6, so memory and the SSD cache hold about three times as many conversations' states. Outputs from a restored state can differ at near ties, as from any change of arithmetic.
- **A reply's state is kept at the page its retained tokens reach**, so the conversation's next turn resumes there instead of prefilling the reply again.

## Fixes

- **Sessions that arrive together decode together** when the Mac's free memory sits at the host's 1 GiB margin: a request starting beside one in service takes that margin when the headroom holds all it and the lanes in service are projected to take, never the spare reserve, and waits as before while macOS warns. On a 48 GB M5 Pro beside an 11 GiB load, two Qwen3.8-27B sessions of about 30K tokens decode 1.49× the tokens a second, as they no longer take turns (#758).
- **A clean stop leaves nothing in the persistent cache that the next start cannot take back**: the KV copies no state needs, as a block a reply completes past its restore point or the chain of a state a full SSD quota gave up, go before the files sync, where 1.6.1 and 1.6.2 left them behind.
- **A persistent cache's directory survives an interrupted system call** at a start, retrying it a bounded number of times where a signal made it fail (#772, David Kornahrens).
- **The SSD quota counts the blocks a failed hole punch leaves**, as 1.6.2's notes said it did.
- **A cached state holding a NaN or an infinity restores as NaN**, where its block could restore as finite values.
- **Under `--verify-rows all` a lane verifies the same rows whatever output it has left**, so an answer `max_tokens` cuts is the start of a longer one, as in 1.6.2.
- **The step costs a start measures are logged**, and a measurement whose repeats disagree by more than 20% is not kept, so the next start measures them again.
- **A start whose Neural Engine split fails to prepare again keeps its calibration** and serves the GPU alone that once; the split is timed on the layers its fit used.
- **A tokenizer whose added tokens are normalized**, as `add_tokens` adds them, which 1.6.2 refused to start, is served again; a client's text still never encodes as one of them.
- **A message sent in an existing chat stays in it when the app quits before the server takes it**, with its attachments; a new chat's first message, held back, stops from whichever chat is open.

## Server

- **The Started line names the effort a request's `chat_template_kwargs` set** where the template reads it: `effort low (chat_template_kwargs)` (#777).
- **`--host unix:PATH` removes only a stale socket at its path**, and refuses a start whose bind a link put at the path redirected.

## Upgrade

```bash
brew upgrade splash
```

Keep the CLI and desktop app on the same release.

- **Persistent caches start cold once.** A cache that 1.6.x wrote is under another namespace, as its states' format changed, so every conversation's first turn after the upgrade prefills its prompt again; its next turns resume from 1.7.0's cache. 1.6.x's files stay beside the new ones, count against the same limit and go 14 days after their last use, or sooner when the limit needs their room.
- **`--state-format bf16` no longer shrinks cached states**: they are int8 codes in either format.
- **A model's first start on each version of Splash and of macOS measures its decode steps**, which later starts read back: on a 48 GB M5 Pro, Qwen3.8-27B's decode warmups take 11.1 s at a first start and 1.0 s at a later one.
- **A request whose KV spills to the SSD reads through a larger hot block cache**: at least 585 MiB for a decoding request, where 1.6.2 kept 390, as its lane verifies up to 16 rows.
- **Greedy and seeded outputs can differ from run to run** under `--verify-rows auto`, the default, which picks each cycle's rows by measured costs; `--verify-rows all` keeps them repeatable.
- **`/status`** keeps its schema and gains `state.idle_cached_cells`.
