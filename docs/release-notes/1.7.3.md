# Splash 1.7.3

Splash 1.7.3 serves Inkling-2-Mini's images from each of its sources: Unsloth's and ggml-org's GGUF projectors, its release's weights and its MLX conversions, oMLX's and nvfp4 ones among them. An Inkling-2-Mini installation that 1.7.2 made, text only, takes its tower in at its next start that reaches the Hub. `mlx-community/Inkling-2-Mini-4bit` loads: 1.7.2 refused it.

## Inkling-2-Mini's images

- **Its vision tower** runs on the dense attention and linear kernels that Qwen's tower uses. These gain a head of 64, a residual scaled by a LayerScale, and a LayerNorm with an F32 output. The tower has 23 blocks, 1,024 wide, with 16 heads of 64, 20-pixel patches, a class token, an axial 2D RoPE and LayerScale, and merges 2 x 2 into a projector without biases that outputs the text's 2,304. Measured against an fp32 tower of the same BF16 weights, its embeddings are within 0.7% to 1.7%, and its worst token within 2.0%, at the six grids measured, of 4 to 4,000 patches (2 x 2, 2 x 128, 128 x 2, 6 x 124, 20 x 200 and 60 x 60), padded or not. That reference matched transformers' `Inkling2VisionModel` to within 1e-5.
- **Each source writes the same image bytes from the same BF16 values**:
  - GGUF repositories: an `inkling2v` projector. The installer prefers BF16, then F32 and F16, and takes Q8_0 last; with ggml-org's Q8_0 projector, the embeddings are within 1.84% of the same fp32 tower of the BF16 weights.
  - The release's checkpoint (`model.visual.*`).
  - An MLX conversion's tower under any of three names: transformers' (`model.vision_tower.*`, as `mlx-community/Inkling-2-Mini-4bit` keeps it), oMLX's (the release's names under `vision_tower.`, as `mlx-community/Inkling-2-Mini-oQ4e-mtp` keeps them), or an nvfp4 conversion's (transformers' block names under `vision_tower.`, beside the release's position embedding and projector, as `thoddnn/Inkling-2-Mini-nvfp4` keeps them).

  A config may name the patch and merge sizes as the release does or as transformers saves them.
- **An image keeps its size.** It is padded right and bottom to whole 40-pixel tokens, with the reference's -1, which the encoder writes past the image's own height and width. A 1000 x 620 screenshot takes 400 tokens. Only an image past the patch budget is resized, its sides alike: `--max-image-pixels` / 256 patches, which is 4,096 tokens or about 6.5 megapixels at the default. A transparent image is composited onto the processor's checkerboard after any resize.
- **A tower the engine cannot prepare is refused when it is installed, not at every start.** Once a tower's files have downloaded, the installer has the engine prepare it, as a start does; this takes under a second. A Qwen tower the engine refuses is refused naming `--language-only`; Inkling-2-Mini serves text instead (below). Such a tower is a quantized MLX one, uses names the engine does not bind, or holds a weight that is not exactly a BF16.
- **Inkling-2-Mini falls back to text, as 1.7.2 served it.** It does so where its tower is refused, and where a source has no tower Splash serves: a GGUF repository without a projector, or a checkpoint without its tower. The installation records why, and later starts do not ask again until the repository's commit moves.

## Inkling-2-Mini on 24 GB Macs

- **The desktop app suggests `unsloth/Inkling-2-Mini-GGUF:UD-IQ3_XXS` to 24 GB Macs**: 13 GB with its projector and its draft. On a 24 GB M6 its experts stay in memory, it serves 160,785 tokens of context with its vision tower loaded (197,649 with `--language-only`), decodes 68 to 79 tokens a second on a code prompt and 53 to 59 on prose (256-token answers), prefills a 2,890-token prompt in 2.6 s, and passes the same 48 image checks as on larger Macs, the oracle and the agent clients' smoke (Claude Code and Hermes).

## Fixes

- **Inkling-2-Mini starts on a 16 GB M5** (#815). On an M5 whose GPU has 8 to 14 cores, a start at one lane failed in its decode warmup with "projection gate scratch buffer holds 114688 bytes, needs 221184", in 1.7.2 too. A 16 GB M5 serves `unsloth/Inkling-2-Mini-GGUF:UD-Q2_K_XL` at one lane, and `--max-concurrent-requests 1` asks for one on any Mac. At one lane the draft's gate/up pair runs a tile of 16 rows where it takes two passes, and its scratch was sized for the 8 rows a plan of the pair's shape alone stores. The scratch now holds the rows of any tile the device may run: 0.1 MB more at one lane, and at three lanes on M3 and M4 GPUs. By the engine's plans the same start failed for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B and Flint on M5 GPUs of 8, 12 and 14 cores, for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on 8, 10 and 14, and for Ornith-1.5-122B on 8, 10, 12 and 16, an M5 Pro's; Qwen3.8-27B and a server of two lanes or more never did. No Mac this release was measured on has an M5 GPU of fewer than 16 cores: on a 16-core M5 Pro with its core count forced to 8, 10, 12 and 14, this release's earlier build fails with the report's own message where the plans say so, and this one starts and answers Inkling-2-Mini and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, GGUF and MLX, and at one lane passes their HTTP smoke and 48 image checks (Inkling-2-Mini at 10 cores, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B at 8). Ornith-1.5-122B's start at 16 cores was not run.
- **`mlx-community/Inkling-2-Mini-4bit` loads.** 1.7.2 refused it with "message gates the fused kernels do not take". That conversion keeps a message gate's down projection in BF16 and its forget and gain in F32, and the fused message gates take a call's gates in one format. A call whose gates mix BF16 and F32 now has its BF16 rows widened to F32, exactly; this takes 28 MB more for that conversion's 48 gates. A call of one format keeps it, so the release's BF16 gates load as in 1.7.2.
- **A GGUF whose message gates mix BF16 with another format loads.** The two smallest quants of `kaitchup/Inkling-2-Mini-dondon-GGUF` keep some gates BF16 beside Q8_0 ones: they installed and then failed every start, in 1.7.2 too. Such a call's BF16 gates are widened to F32, exactly, as a checkpoint's are. Of 437 Inkling-2-Mini GGUFs surveyed on the Hub, these two mix them.
- **The console's Started line leaves thinking out for Inkling-2-Mini and Flint** (#814). It printed "thinking off" for every Inkling-2-Mini request, though the model reasoned. Those models open their own reasoning, so the line now names the effort alone. Qwen's lines are unchanged, and no output changes.
- **An image's memory is counted as Metal allocates it**, in 16 KiB pages. The image cache, its admission and its telemetry counted an image's values, up to a page short for each buffer, which Inkling's 20-pixel patches make the rule.
- **An image that cannot be decoded no longer ends a Claude Code session.** Claude Code's Read tool sends a file cut short as an image, which stays in the conversation: Splash answered 400 "image could not be decoded" to that request and to every later one of the session. It now answers "Could not process image", as Anthropic's API does, on which Claude Code takes the image out with a note and goes on. This holds for Qwen's images as for Inkling's, and for formats Splash does not decode (HEIC, BMP, TIFF).
- **A Claude Code session that gathers more than 64 images goes on.** A request holds at most 64 images, and Claude Code keeps up to 100 in a conversation: the 65th got 400 "requests support at most 64 images", as did every request after it. A Messages request of more image blocks is now refused naming its oldest one, as Anthropic's API names a block, and Claude Code takes an image out and sends the request again. In a session of 74 images read one a turn, every image past the 64th was answered from, at one refused request a turn; Claude Code then keeps the first 64 images and a note in place of each later one, so the model may read a later image again when asked about it.
- **Image and PDF data accept base64 that is wrapped, unpadded or URL-safe.** `base64` on Linux wraps its output at 76 columns, and those requests got 400 "not valid base64".
- **A long conversation of short turns reaches its context on Inkling-2-Mini and Flint** (#824). These models' KV pages that every attention window has passed are trimmed to a fraction of their size, a sixth on Inkling-2-Mini, and the memory plan prices a context so. Each cached state keeps the window of pages before it whole, so that a request can resume there, and a state the SSD cache kept went on holding them for as long as its conversation ran: nothing took such a state on a running request's own chain. In an agent's session, whose turns are shorter than a window and leave two states each, every page after the first turn stayed whole. The server then evicted every other cached prompt, shrank a streamed expert store to its floor, and ended the request with `capacity_exhausted ... could not allocate KV target` at a fraction of its context, in 1.7.2 too. On a 36 GB M5 Max with default options, Inkling-2-Mini UD-Q4_K_M failed at 101,024 tokens of its 262,144; on a 48 GB M5 Pro held to 29 GB at 121,931, and held to 12 GB at 68,274; UD-IQ3_XXS on a 24 GB M6 held to 15 GB at 24,449 of the 87,057 its plan gave. Where the memory budget now refuses a request's pages, the states behind its window leave first, oldest first, and their pages are trimmed; the state at the prompt's head and the request's newest stay. The same conversations run to 261,574 tokens on the M5 Max and the M5 Pro and to 86,825 on the M6, and end at the context limit. With memory to spare no state leaves, and a single long prompt allocates as before. A request that goes back to an earlier turn whose state has left resumes at the nearest state before it and computes the rest again.

## Speed

Against 1.7.2's build, in paired runs on an idle Mac (1.7.2's build, this release's, this release's, 1.7.2's):

- **Text is as fast as in 1.7.2, and its outputs are the same.** In the release check's runs every output token, the draft's acceptance and the weight images' bytes are equal:
  - Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on a 16-core M5 Pro (48 GB). MLX 4-bit runs a decode step of one row in 19.44 ms against 19.49 and of four rows in 29.05 against 29.06, and prefills 14,096 tokens cold in 3,889 ms against 3,889. UD-Q4_K_M runs them in 22.94 ms against 22.94 and 32.58 against 32.50, and prefills in 4,882 ms against 4,835 (0.97%, inside the runs' 1.17% noise).
  - Qwen3.6-35B-A3B UD-Q2_K_XL on a 24 GB M6: 25.55 ms against 25.60 and 41.50 against 41.49, and 6,648 ms against 6,679.
  - Qwen3.8-27B on a 20-core M5 Pro (64 GB), with one Neural Engine share of the prefill given to both builds (`ANE_FFN_SHARE`), as the check's own calibration differed between its rounds. UD-Q4_K_M runs a decode step of one row in 78.11 ms against 79.08 and of four rows in 93.51 against 94.54, and prefills 14,096 tokens cold in 19,207 ms against 19,398. MLX 4-bit runs a step of four rows in 84.83 ms against 85.63 and prefills in 15,125 ms against 15,031 (0.63%, inside the runs' 1.19% noise). Its step of one row was inconclusive there, 1.7.2's own two rounds being 5% apart, and took 68.42 ms against 68.44 in the release check's own run, whose decode rounds agreed.
  - Inkling-2-Mini UD-Q4_K_M, text only, on the 48 GB Mac. The check's benchmark holds prompts of Qwen's tokens, so its text was compared by paired runs of `decode-profile`: it prefills 2,583 tokens in 1,735 ms against 1,729 and runs a decode cycle (the draft's block, every row verified) in 44.3 ms against 44.4, medians of four pairs, with the same output digest in every run. With its tower loaded its text is as fast (1,732 ms and 44.5 ms, measured on the image commit).
  - On a 24 GB M6 under `--verify-rows all`, Inkling-2-Mini UD-IQ3_XXS gives 32 requests the same answers on both builds, with its tower and without.
  - Ternary-Bonsai-2-27B (PQ2_0) and Flint (GGUF and MLX) were measured so on this release's image commit, after which no kernel changed: within 1%, their output digests equal.
- **Qwen's images are as fast.** Measured on the image commit: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B's tower encodes 1,600 patches in 101.8 ms against 101.7 and 4,096 in 368.7 against 368.6, and a served request with an image reaches its first token in 474 ms in both.
- **Inkling-2-Mini's tower** encodes 1,600 patches (a 640 x 1000 image) in 70 ms, 4,096 (1280 x 1280) in 241 ms, and the largest image it takes, 16,384 patches (2560 x 2560), in 2.5 s, where attention is four fifths of the time. Served, a 1000 x 700 screenshot of 483 prompt tokens adds about 0.4 s to the first token.

## Upgrade

```bash
brew upgrade splash
```

Keep the CLI and desktop app on the same release.

- **An Inkling-2-Mini installation from 1.7.2 takes its tower in at its next start that reaches the Hub.** A GGUF repository's projector downloads (0.64 GB at BF16). Offline it goes on serving text. A projector that cannot download is tried again at each start that reaches the Hub, with a warning, and the installation serves text meanwhile. An installation pinned to a commit with `--revision` takes its tower in too: until it has, each start asks the Hub for that commit's listing. `--language-only` keeps it text only.
- **The desktop app starts models offline**, so it shows a notice on its Server page for such an installation, that it is installed text only, with an Update button that takes the tower in. Check for Updates in the model's menu does the same. Where the tower cannot download, the app says it could not update the model, and the notice stays.
- **Inkling-2-Mini's tower loads with the model and takes memory from the context.** Where memory bounds the context, the context limit is lower than without the tower: on a 24 GB M6, UD-IQ3_XXS serves 160,785 tokens with it and 197,649 without. `--language-only` gives the difference back. On Macs that hold the model's whole 262,144-token window either way, nothing changes.
- **Every GGUF installation is assembled again at its first start**, because the installer's GGUF metadata changed. Its persistent SSD cache therefore starts cold once.
- **The desktop app's suggestion of Inkling-2-Mini no longer says "Text only".**
- **The engine protocol is version 16.** An image span now carries the image's own height and width, past which Inkling's encoder pads. The status schema stays at 8.

## Known issues

- **A quantized MLX vision tower is not served.** An Inkling-2-Mini conversion with one serves text only and says why at installation; a Qwen one is refused naming `--language-only`. mlx-community's, oMLX's and the nvfp4 conversions keep their towers in BF16.
- **Inkling-2-Mini's MTP head is not loaded**, as in 1.7.2: its DFlash2 draft drafts.
- **Inkling-2-Mini reasons at `reasoning_effort` `none`** too, as in 1.7.2: 50 to 140 tokens on a one-word question, and `--thinking-token-budget`, `--thinking-end-text` and a request's `thinking_token_budget` do not act on it. A client must leave room for the reasoning in a request's output limit, which counts its tokens: a short limit can end a response before its answer starts.
- **A Responses chain is not stored once its history passes 64 MiB.** The store keeps each response with its whole input history, its images' base64 included, within 64 MiB in all. A response whose history alone passes that reports `"store": false`, and a request that continues from it gets 404 `previous_response_not_found`, on which a client resends its history. A chain that sends a 1600 x 1200 PNG screenshot each turn, 6.6 MiB of base64, reaches it at its tenth turn, as in 1.7.2. The chain's older responses leave the store sooner, the first at the fourth such turn, which matters only to a client that branches from one. A client that sends the conversation itself is not affected.
- **A request holds at most 64 images**, PDF pages included, as in 1.7.2. Claude Code goes on past them as above, keeping the first 64; another client's request of more gets 400.
- **HEIC, BMP and TIFF images are not decoded**, as in 1.7.2: they get 400 `Could not process image`. An iPhone's photos are HEIC by default.
- **Qwen3.6-35B-A3B misreads large text in an image that is not resized**, as in 1.7.2. An image whose sides are both multiples of 32 pixels reaches its tower pixel for pixel, and the model then misreads sharp text set at 64 pixels or more: on a 1280 x 800 image it read each of ten texts set at 32 pixels and none of the thirty at 64, 96 and 144, in five fonts. On a 1270 x 790 image, which is resized, it read every one. The pixels and the tower's embeddings match the reference implementation (transformers', within 5e-5 in fp32), which points at the model; it has not been run in another runtime. Of those thirty, Qwen3.8-27B read 28 and Inkling-2-Mini all. An image a few pixels narrower is read right.
- **`gguf-projection` can fail at random under Metal shader validation on an M5 Pro** (#807), as since 1.7.1: the validator reads some of the staged tile's in-bounds weight prefetches, loaded behind a condition, as zero. Outputs without validation are right. Loading them on every step passes there but fails the suite under validation on an M4, so 1.7.3 keeps 1.7.1's loads.
